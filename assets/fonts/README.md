# Figtree

`figtree-latin.woff2` and `figtree-latin-ext.woff2` are the latin and latin-ext
subsets of [Figtree](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Figtree) by Erik Kennedy,
licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1
(<https://openfontlicense.org>).

They are variable-weight files covering 300–900, downloaded once from Google
Fonts (v9) and committed here so the page loads no third-party resources.
